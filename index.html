1967

The Carol Burnett Show

Vicki Lawrence joins the landmark variety series.

1973

Music breaks through

Her recording of “The Night the Lights Went Out in Georgia” reaches No. 1 in the United States and Canada.

1976

Primetime Emmy

Lawrence wins the Primetime Emmy for Individual Performance in a Variety or Music Program.

1983

Mama's Family

Thelma Harper moves from sketch comedy into her own television series.

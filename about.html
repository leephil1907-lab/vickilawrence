02 / The story

Behind
the character.

Vicki Lawrence is an American actress, comedian and singer whose career began in 1967 and grew through television, music, comedy and live performance.

THE ACTRESS

Television was the stage.

Her work on The Carol Burnett Show established a versatile performer capable of moving between characters, sketch comedy and dramatic timing.

THE PERFORMER

Mama became legacy.

Thelma Harper, better known as Mama, became one of Lawrence's defining creations and later centered Mama's Family.
